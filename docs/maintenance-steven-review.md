# Maintenance Review Checklist

Use this with Steven before rolling the maintenance intelligence workflow out broadly.

## Safety Copy

- Electrical: confirm PPE, lockout/tagout language, and stop conditions.
- Plumbing: confirm sewage/biohazard escalation wording.
- Biohazard: confirm cleanup steps match current school policy.
- Grounds/pond care: confirm label/SDS, runoff, wildlife, and local-rule warnings.

## Workflow

- Split-ticket flow: confirm managers can separate mixed work orders quickly.
- Closeout flow: confirm the fields match how techs describe completed work.
- Photo documentation: confirm the required completion photo is practical.
- Repeat repairs: confirm the replacement-vs-repair recommendation threshold feels right.

## Pond Calculator

- Confirm the volume methods are enough: known gallons, rectangle, circle, surface acres.
- Confirm product concentration and target dose labels match the products currently used.
- Confirm staff should attach a product label or SDS before applying chemicals.
